# North Georgia Holiday Events
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User specified React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind, FastAPI, uv, SQLite, Leaflet/OpenStreetMap, and Docker Compose on Synology DS1821+.
## Users
Jason and LAN visitors finding holiday events; one administrator maintains the calendar.
## Product Purpose
Find Christmas events and concerts across North Georgia using listings, a map, and a calendar. Support future years without code changes.
## Operating Context
LAN-only initially. Outbound internet permitted for maps, geocoding and source research. Manual event updates. No cloud account or paid API.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Public read-only views; one protected administrator. Import/export CSV and JSON. Preserve unverified entries with public labels. Never invent coordinates or uncertain times and prices. Cache geocoding permanently; provide manual coordinate correction. Eastern Time.
## Brand Commitments
North Georgia Holiday Events. Jason approved a warm, understated holiday guide: cream background, evergreen navigation, cranberry accents, list beside the map.
## Evidence on Hand
The pasted requirements supply the initial November/December 2026 event list. Official sources must support confirmation. No supplied imagery or brand assets.
## Product Principles
- Make dates and uncertainty clear.
- Keep application data local and portable.
- Make future seasons editable rather than hard-coded.
